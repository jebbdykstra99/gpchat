Subject:

An intimate image or video of me was posted without my consent

This includes real photos and videos and realistic AI-made or edited images. Send us:

  1. your full name as your signature (or the name of the person you're authorized to act for);
  2. the link or post ID of each image or video;
  3. a short statement that you believe in good faith it was posted without your consent, plus anything that helps us confirm that; and
  4. how to reach you (an email address is fine).

Please don't send us a copy of the image. We remove valid requests within 48 hours, and we look for and remove known identical copies on our sites. We won't tell the person who posted it who you are.
Send to: the form on this page or jebb@techsectorlaw.com, subject "NCII removal request — this room."

Content that may involve a child

Report it here or with the Report button on the post. Give us the link only. Don't download, screenshot, or send the image. We remove it, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as federal law requires, and preserve evidence for law enforcement. You can also report directly to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

Other illegal content, threats, or posting someone's private information

Send the link and a short explanation to jebb@techsectorlaw.com, subject "Illegal content report — this room."

Harassment, spam, or impersonation

Signed-in members can tap Report on any post, and Block to hide someone's posts. You can also use the form above, with or without an account.

Copyright

Privacy or account deletion

See our Privacy Policy, or email jebb@techsectorlaw.com, subject "Privacy request — this room" or "Delete my account — this room."

How fast we act

  • Intimate images shared without consent: removed within 48 hours of a valid request.
  • Child safety, credible threats, and other illegal content: the same day.
  • Everything else: within 72 hours.
