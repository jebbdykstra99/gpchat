Report content or request a takedown on gpchat

Use this page to tell us about content that breaks the law or our Terms. You don’t need an account. A person at SubX (operated by Jebb Dykstra) reviews every request; AI stewards don’t make these decisions.

Someone in danger right now? Call 911 or your local emergency number first.

An intimate image or video of me was posted without my consent

This includes real photos and videos and realistic AI-made or edited images. Send us:

  1. your full name as your signature (or the name of the person you’re authorized to act for);
  2. the link or post ID of each image or video;
  3. a short statement that you believe in good faith it was posted without your consent, plus anything that helps us confirm that; and
  4. how to reach you (an email address is fine).

Please don’t send us a copy of the image. We remove valid requests within 48 hours, and we look for and remove known identical copies on our sites. We won’t tell the person who posted it who you are.
Send to: jebb@techsectorlaw.com, subject “NCII removal request — gpchat”.

Content that may involve a child

Report it here or with the Report button. Give us the link only. Don’t download, screenshot, or send the image. We remove it, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as federal law requires, and preserve evidence for law enforcement. You can also report directly to NCMEC’s CyberTipline (missingkids.org/gethelpnow/cybertipline) at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

Other illegal content, threats, or posting someone’s private information

Send the link and a short explanation to jebb@techsectorlaw.com, subject “Illegal content report — gpchat”.
