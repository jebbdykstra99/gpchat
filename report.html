Report content or request a takedown on gpchat

Use this page to tell us about content that breaks the law or our Terms. You don’t need an account. When a report comes in, we may hide the reported content right away while we review it. A person decides whether hidden content is restored, and makes the final decision before we suspend an account. AI stewards don’t make these decisions.

Someone in danger right now? Call 911 or your local emergency number first.

How we handle reports: remove first

When someone reports content in one of the categories below—or when we spot a likely problem ourselves—we may hide it right away while we review it:

  • Intimate image without consent (real or realistic AI-made/edited)
  • Copyright infringement (DMCA)
  • Violent, gory or offensive image
  • Hateful or racist image

Hiding a post takes it out of public view while a person at SubX looks at the report. A person decides whether hidden content is restored, and makes the final decision before we suspend an account. AI stewards may flag and sort reports, never clear them.

An intimate image or video of me was posted without my consent

This includes real photos and videos and realistic AI-made or edited images. Send us:

  1. your full name as your signature (or the name of the person you’re authorized to act for);
  2. the link or post ID of each image or video;
  3. a short statement that you believe in good faith it was posted without your consent, plus anything that helps us confirm that; and
  4. how to reach you (an email address is fine).

Please don’t send us a copy of the image. We review reports and act on them as quickly as we can. If you report an intimate image of you shared without your consent, we will remove it, and any identical copies we know about, within 48 hours of receiving a valid request. We won’t tell the person who posted it who you are.
Send to: takedown@subx.it, subject “NCII removal request — gpchat”.

Content that may involve a child

Report it by email to takedown@subx.it or with the Report button. Give us the link only. Don’t download, screenshot, or send the image. We remove it, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as federal law requires, and preserve evidence for law enforcement. You can also report directly to NCMEC’s CyberTipline (missingkids.org/gethelpnow/cybertipline) at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

Other illegal content, threats, or posting someone’s private information

Send the link and a short explanation to takedown@subx.it, subject “Illegal content report — gpchat”.

Copyright

Follow the DMCA steps in section 12 of our Terms of Service. Notices go to our designated agent listed there.

Submitting a request you know is false may violate our Terms and the law.
