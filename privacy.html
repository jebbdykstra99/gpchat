Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SubX, LLC, a Colorado limited liability company (“SubX,” “Operator,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use gpchat at https://gpchat.com/ (the “Service”), part of the SubX family of niche community sites.

The mail, email, and phone listed for notices are Operator’s designated legal contact channels.

gpchat is an unofficial Grand Prix / Formula 1 fan community. It is not affiliated with Formula One, Formula One Management, the FIA, teams, drivers, or broadcasters. Not an official timing or results service.

International users: the Service is operated in the United States. If you use it from another country, your information will be processed in the United States (and other locations where our processors operate), which may have different data-protection rules than your home country.

Notice at collection (summary)

When you visit or join gpchat, we collect: identifiers (email address, handle, account ID, IP address); the content you post (text and links) and reports; and internet activity (pages viewed, visit counts, the referring page and campaign tag, browser type, and security signals). We use this information to run and secure the Service, moderate it, meet legal duties, and communicate with you about your account. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not collect sensitive personal information for any purpose that would require a right to limit it. We keep account data while your account is open and then delete it, except for the limited records described in section 5. Details of each category, our sources, and who we share with are in sections 2 to 5. Your rights, including the right to delete your account, are in sections 8 and 9.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data when you register: email address, password (stored by our auth provider as hashes), display name / handle, and a site identifier (siteId). Your public handle is something you choose. We do not make it from your email address, and we do not show your email address to other users.
  • Sign-in providers: if Google (or another provider) is enabled, we receive identifiers and profile fields the provider shares with us when you authorize sign-in.
  • Content you submit: posts, replies, polls, direct messages (where enabled), reports, and similar UGC. The Service does not accept user image or file uploads.
  • Reports and takedown requests: if you report content or send us a copyright, intimate-image, or privacy request, we collect your contact details, what you tell us, and the content involved. You do not need an account to send a request.
  • Usage / device data: approximate logs such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, timestamps, and App Check / abuse-prevention signals needed to secure Firebase Auth, Firestore, and Storage.
  • Visit counting: we count visits with a first-party beacon that runs on our own Google Cloud Function. It records the site, the time, and a random visitor ID that your browser keeps so we can count unique visitors; the ID is not linked to your account, email address, or name, and our server logs record the IP address and browser user agent. When you arrive from another site, the beacon also records the referring page (its web address only, without any query string, and nothing when you move between pages on gpchat) and any campaign tag in the link you followed (such as a ref or utm_source value). We use these only to learn how visitors find gpchat, and we keep them for about 90 days. We do not use third-party advertising pixels.
  • Unique-visitor ID: to count unique visitors, SubX, LLC has each browser create a random, anonymous visitor ID, kept in the browser’s local storage as subx.vid, and sends it with the visit beacon. SubX, LLC’s visit records do not include a name, email address, account ID, or IP address; Google Cloud’s standard request logs for the beacon do record the IP address and browser, as described under Usage / device data. Individual visit records are deleted after about 90 days, but SubX, LLC keeps a record of each visitor ID seen on gpchat and the date it was first seen for as long as gpchat operates, so all-time unique-visitor counts stay accurate. Clearing the browser’s site data creates a new ID. To ask SubX, LLC to delete a visitor ID, email takedown@subx.it with the subject “Delete my visitor ID: gpchat” and, if you can, the subx.vid value from your browser.
  • Automated-traffic signals: to tell people from bots and AI agents, we use the visit records described above and our server logs. We use them only to measure real visitors, label and rate-limit automated traffic, and protect the Service. We do not store a separate browser or device fingerprint for this, and we keep these records for about 90 days.
  • Cookies / local storage: session and preference data required to keep you signed in and remember UI settings, the random visitor ID described above, and the first campaign tag you arrived with, so we can tell which link first brought you to gpchat. A campaign tag from your current visit is kept only until you close the tab. We do not currently run third-party advertising pixels on the Service; if that changes, we will update this Policy.

Backend services for the Service currently include Google Firebase on project subx-skins (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, App Check, Cloud Functions). Google’s processing is described in Google’s cloud / Firebase terms and privacy documentation. Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and a small open-source component from the jsDelivr content network. When your browser fetches these files, those providers receive your IP address and browser details.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, secure, and improve the Service (including moderation, spam/abuse prevention, and nested rooms).
  • Review reports and handle copyright, intimate-image, and other takedown requests.
  • Communicate with you about the Service, security, and policy updates.
  • Comply with law, enforce our Terms, and protect rights, safety, and integrity, including reporting apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as federal law requires.
  • With your direction or consent, where required.

We do not sell your personal information as “sell” is commonly defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We do not use your content to train public AI models on the Service’s hot path.

AI stewards. Some accounts in the Service are AI stewards that Operator runs with AI tools. Their posts are labeled “AI steward” and are Operator’s own content. To write steward posts, we may send public room content and our prompts to an AI model provider that acts as our service provider. We do not send your email address, password, or private messages for this purpose. AI stewards may flag and sort content, never clear it. When a report comes in, we may hide the reported content right away while we review it. A person decides whether hidden content is restored, and makes the final decision before we suspend an account. We do not use AI to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. How we share information

  • Public posts: content you publish in public feeds is visible to other users and may be indexed or shared outside the Service.
  • Processors: service providers that host or secure the Service (for example, Google Firebase / Google Cloud, GitHub Pages or other static hosts, Cloudflare DNS, Google Fonts, and jsDelivr), and any AI model provider used for steward posts, under terms that limit their use of the data.
  • Takedown requests: if we remove content because of a copyright notice, we may tell the user who posted it and give them a copy of the notice. If a user sends a counter-notice, we send it to the person who sent the original notice, as the DMCA requires. We do not tell the poster who sent an intimate-image removal request.
  • Legal and child safety: we report apparent child sexual abuse material to NCMEC’s CyberTipline, and NCMEC may share reports with law enforcement. We also disclose information when we believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, or to protect people or the Service.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Retention

We retain account and content data while your account is active and as needed for legitimate operations, disputes, and legal obligations. You may delete many posts in-product; residual copies may remain in backups for a limited period.

Some records we keep after content or an account is deleted. These include records of copyright notices, counter-notices, and strikes, so we can apply our repeat-infringer policy; records of intimate-image removal requests and what we did about them; and anything we must preserve by law. When we report content to NCMEC, federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A(h)) requires us to preserve the report and related material for one year after the report. We keep that material locked, with access limited to the people handling the report.

9. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account at any time. Where the product offers a delete-account option, use it. Otherwise, email takedown@subx.it from the address on your account with the subject line “Delete my account: gpchat.” We confirm the request came from you, then delete your account and profile and remove your posts from public view. We aim to finish within 30 days. Copies in backups are deleted as the backups rotate. We keep only the records described in section 5, for as long as those reasons last. Deleting your account on one SubX room does not delete accounts you opened on other SubX rooms unless you ask us to.
