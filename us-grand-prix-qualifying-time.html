What time is US Grand Prix qualifying? Saturday Oct 24, 4:00 PM Central

Qualifying for the 2026 United States Grand Prix is Saturday Oct 24 at 4:00 PM Central (CT) at Circuit of the Americas, Austin. That's 3:00 PM Mountain (MT), 5:00 PM Eastern (ET), 2:00 PM Pacific (PT) and 21:00 UTC. Austin is a standard weekend this year, so there's no Sprint or Sprint Qualifying.
