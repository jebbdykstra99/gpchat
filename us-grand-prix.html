US Grand Prix 2026 at COTA, Austin: start times and fan chat

Formula 1 is at Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas, Friday Oct 23 to Sunday Oct 25. It's a standard weekend with no Sprint: three practice sessions, qualifying on Saturday, and the race on Sunday. The race is 56 laps of the 5.513 km circuit (308.405 km), per the formula1.com race page.

Race start time: Sunday Oct 25 at 3:00 PM Central (CT) in Austin, which is 2:00 PM Mountain (MT), 4:00 PM Eastern (ET), 1:00 PM Pacific (PT) and 20:00 UTC.

When does FP1 start in Denver (Mountain Time)?

Practice 1 starts Friday, Oct 23, at 11:30 AM Mountain (MDT), which is 12:30 PM Central in Austin. The race starts Sunday, Oct 25, at 2:00 PM Mountain. (Source: Jolpica round 18 and formula1.com, already cited on the page.)

Is there a Sprint in Austin this year?

No. Austin is a standard weekend in 2026: three practice sessions, qualifying on Saturday and the race on Sunday.
