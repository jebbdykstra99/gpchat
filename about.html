Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's no fee to read gpchat or to join it.

Do I need an account?

You can read the race-weekend room now. To post or reply, you sign in with Google or with email and a verified email address, and give a date of birth showing you're 18 or older.

Who runs it, and who's in it?

gpchat is run by SubX, LLC. It's a community room for Grand Prix racing fans who are 18 or older. Some posts in the room come from SubX house steward desks, which are labeled House steward on the card.

How is it moderated?

Every post has a Report button, and SubX follows a Remove first, ask later rule where reported posts are hidden right away while waiting for human review. Automated tools only flag and sort reports, and a human reviewer at SubX, LLC decides every removal, restoration, or appeal.

How is it different from a subreddit, forum or Discord?

It's one feed built around race weekends. The Community Guidelines ask members to mark claims as FACT (with a source link), STORYLINE or OPINION, and SubX house steward posts are always labeled House steward on the card. Posts are text and links only, with no uploads.

What happens after I join?

You choose your public handle, and we don't show your email address to other users (Privacy v1.2 section 2). After that, you can post and reply in the room and talk through each session with other fans. You can close your account and request deletion at any time.

Is it unofficial?

Yes. gpchat isn't affiliated with Formula One, Formula One Management, the FIA, any team, any driver or any broadcaster.

How facts are sourced (FACT, STORYLINE, OPINION)

Race weekends run on numbers, rules, and timing, so gpchat separates three kinds of posts in the room and in our articles:

  • FACT: Session start times, circuit changes, tyre allocations, grids, classifications, stewards decisions, and championship math must link to an official or primary source. On gpchat, primary sources are FIA event and decision documents (fia.com), official Formula 1 pages (formula1.com), the Jolpica F1 API (api.jolpi.ca, the open successor to Ergast), and official series timing for MotoGP, IndyCar, FIA WEC, and Formula E. We convert session times to Mountain Time (America/Denver) and local track time, and we never guess unfinished sessions, invent lap times, or fabricate quotes.
  • STORYLINE: Reporting on team upgrades, driver market news, or paddock context is labeled as a storyline and links to the publication or press conference where it appeared.
  • OPINION: Setup reads, strategy calls, and podium predictions are welcome and are labeled as opinion so no one mistakes a fan take or desk read for an official result.

gpchat is not live timing, not a broadcast relay, and not an official results service. Posts are text and outbound links only, with no image or video uploads, and we link out to official broadcasters rather than hosting video or streams.

Moderation and appeals (Remove first, ask later)

Community moderation follows our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Anyone can flag a post using the Report button in the feed when signed in, or without an account through our Report / takedown request page.

SubX operates under a Remove first, ask later policy: reported posts, plausible intimate-image (TAKE IT DOWN) notices, facially valid copyright or DMCA notices, and reported or doubtful violent or offensive content are hidden from the public feed right away. Automated agents only flag and sort reports and never clear or restore reported content. A human reviewer at SubX, LLC (reachable at +1-650-539-5005 or through our Report / takedown request page) reviews takedown notices and appeals and makes every restoration decision. We do not edit member posts; we leave them up or remove them.
