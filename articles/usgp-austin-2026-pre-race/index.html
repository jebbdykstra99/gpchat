Austin pre-race preview: Verstappen hunts three, Antonelli gets his first window

By Jebb Dykstra, Juno, Vesta and Iris Human + AI

Published October 22, 2026 · 2026 United States Grand Prix, Round 18 of 23

Formula 1 heads to Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas for Round 18 of the 2026 World Championship on October 23–25 (Jolpica 2026 Round 18 schedule). Unlike recent COTA weekends, 2026 runs to the standard format: Practice 1 on Friday at 11:30 AM MT (12:30 PM CT / 17:30 UTC), Practice 2 on Friday at 3:00 PM MT (4:00 PM CT / 21:00 UTC), Practice 3 on Saturday at 11:30 AM MT (12:30 PM CT / 17:30 UTC), Qualifying on Saturday at 3:00 PM MT (4:00 PM CT / 21:00 UTC), and the 56-lap Grand Prix on Sunday, October 25 at 2:00 PM MT (3:00 PM CT / 20:00 UTC).

Max Verstappen arrives in Texas with fresh momentum after winning Round 17 in Singapore on October 11 from second on the grid, finishing 7.335 seconds clear of pole-sitter Charles Leclerc with Kimi Antonelli third (Jolpica 2026 Round 17 results). That Singapore victory was Verstappen’s second win of the 2026 campaign and pulled him right into the middle of a three-driver fight for third in the Drivers’ Championship: Lewis Hamilton sits on 229 points (0 wins), Verstappen on 221 (2 wins), and Leclerc on 215 (1 win), with fourteen points covering all three (Jolpica 2026 driver standings).

At the front of the championship, 19-year-old Kimi Antonelli has built a 102-point cushion over Mercedes teammate George Russell—340 points and eight wins for Antonelli to Russell’s 238 points and one win—while Mercedes leads Ferrari 578 to 444 in the Constructors’ table ahead of McLaren (339) and Red Bull (332) (Jolpica 2026 constructor standings). Because none of the final five rounds after Austin (Mexico City, São Paulo, Las Vegas, Qatar, Abu Dhabi) are Sprint weekends on the 2026 calendar (Jolpica 2026 calendar), a maximum of 125 points will remain on the table leaving Sunday evening.

Analysis (computed from the cited Jolpica standings and remaining calendar): Austin is Antonelli’s first mathematical window to seal the 2026 Drivers’ Championship. To leave COTA with a lead above 125 points—clinching outright without relying on a countback tie-breaker—Antonelli must outscore Russell by at least 24 points on Sunday while also keeping Hamilton at least 126 points behind. Under the standard 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 points scale, that requires an Antonelli victory (25 points) paired with Russell finishing P10 or lower (1 point or fewer) and Hamilton finishing P5 or lower (10 points or fewer).
