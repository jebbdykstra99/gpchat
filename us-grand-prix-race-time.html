What time is the US Grand Prix race? Sunday Oct 25, 3:00 PM Central

The 2026 United States Grand Prix starts Sunday Oct 25 at 3:00 PM Central (CT) at Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas. That's 2:00 PM Mountain (MT), 4:00 PM Eastern (ET), 1:00 PM Pacific (PT) and 20:00 UTC. It's 56 laps of the 5.513 km circuit.
